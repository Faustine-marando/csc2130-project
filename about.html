About the project

Home About Contact

This project is about building an online school attendance management system that records student attendance without paperwork.

Our mission

To provide a reliable digital platform for recording, storing, monitoring, and reporting student attendance while reducing manual work, errors, and loss of attendance records.

Our vision

To create an efficient, accurate, and user-friendly student attendance management system that improves attendance tracking and supports better academic administration.

How to use the application

Welcome, and let us walk together through this!

This will be of great help in our universities.

Thanks for joining.

  1. Open your browser and visit the application.
  2. Open the Home page and choose the menu.
  3. Log in or register with the student's name and registration number.
  4. Mark the student's attendance.
